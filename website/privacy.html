What we deliberately do not collect

  • Images of your identity documents. The app stores only the document number you type in. No photo of a passport or national ID is ever uploaded.
  • Card numbers, bank details or mobile-money PINs. Payments run entirely on our payment gateway's own hosted checkout. Those details are entered on the gateway's page and never pass through the Felicek app or our database.
  • The contents of your calls. Audio and video are sent peer-to-peer between the two devices. We store only who called whom, when, and for how long.
  • Advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

Who can see what

  • Your public profile — name, photo, title, bio, skills, trust score, verification badge — is visible to other signed-in users.
  • Your email address, identity document number, wallet balance and block list are visible only to you. These live in a separate record that our security rules make unreadable to every other account.
  • Your full skill-challenge submission — your code, your design, your written answer — is readable only by you. A client sees a score and a short excerpt, never the whole thing.
  • Messages are readable only by the two participants.
These are not policy promises alone — they are enforced by Firestore security rules on the server, and covered by an automated test suite that runs in CI.

Where your data lives

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google acts as our data processor; see the Firebase privacy documentation. Payment processing is handled by our payment gateway as an independent controller for the transaction data it needs.

Your rights

  • Access and portability — request a copy of your data.
  • Correction — edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Deletion — delete your account from the app. We scrub your profile immediately. Financial records and messages already delivered to another person are retained where the law requires.
  • Objection and complaint — contact us, or your local data protection authority.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone — only during a call you start or accept, and for taking a profile photo.
  • Photos — only when you pick a profile photo.
  • Install unknown apps (Android, website builds only) — so the app can install its own updates. Not requested in Play Store builds.

Children

Felicek is not for anyone under 18. Verification is designed to enforce that.

Changes

We will update the date above and, for anything material, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.

1. Accounts and verification

  • You must be 18 or older and provide accurate information.
  • Every account must complete identity verification and clear its mandatory deposit before it can post a job or submit a proposal. This is not optional and cannot be skipped.
  • Individual freelancer accounts must also carry a genuine photograph of the account holder.
  • One account per person or organisation. Impersonation, shared logins and re-registration after a ban are grounds for permanent removal.

2. Deposits

A deposit is not a fee. It is your money. Posting balances are withdrawable whenever you have no open listings; the freelancer trust bond is refunded after your first completed job, or on account closure if you never took work and owe nothing.

3. Fees

Felicek charges a flat platform maintenance fee of 1% on local payouts (bKash, Nagad, bank transfer) and 2% on international payouts (PayPal). There are no listing fees, no bidding fees, no subscription, and no markup on payout rails. Your payment provider may charge its own fees, which we do not control.

4. Escrow and milestones

  • Clients fund milestones before work begins.
  • Funds are released to the freelancer when the client approves a milestone.
  • If a dispute is raised, funds stay held until it is resolved. Both parties agree to engage with the dispute process in good faith.

5. Skill challenges and your work

  • Your challenge submission remains yours. Clients see a score and a short excerpt — never your full code or design — unless and until they hire you. This is enforced technically, not just promised.
  • Attempting to extract another user's submission, or to obtain a quiz answer key, is a serious violation and will end your account.
  • Ownership of delivered work transfers on payment, unless you and the client agree otherwise in writing.

6. Conduct

You agree not to:

  • Send spam or mass-templated proposals. Our filter flags these, and repeat offenders are removed.
  • Take payment off-platform to avoid fees or escrow protection.
  • Harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
  • Post illegal work, or work that requires someone to break the law.
  • Misrepresent your identity, skills or history.
  • Attempt to circumvent verification, deposits, or the app's security controls.

7. Calls and messages

Calls are peer-to-peer; we do not record them. Do not record another person without their consent — in many places that is a criminal offence. Messages are private to the participants, but we may access specific content when required to investigate a report or comply with a legal order.

8. Software updates

Builds distributed from this website update themselves: the app checks for new versions, downloads them, verifies their checksum, and asks Android to install them. Security updates may be marked mandatory, in which case the app requires the update before continuing. By installing from this site you consent to that mechanism.

9. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. You may close yours at any time from the app; deposits and cleared balances you are owed are returned, net of any amount you owe under an open engagement.

10. Disclaimers and liability

Felicek is a marketplace, not a party to the contracts formed on it. We verify identity and hold escrow; we do not guarantee the quality, legality or outcome of any engagement. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the twelve months before the claim.

11. Changes

We will update the date above and notify you in the app before any material change takes effect. Continuing to use Felicek after that means you accept the revised terms.
